## 1. Decisions needed before the batches that reach weeks 41-48

**A. Week 44 (Process Safety) replacement conflict.** Your rule was "Stairways unless already covered; otherwise Hoists." Both are already assigned by the replacements you approved:
- Stairways goes to Week 41.
- Material and Personnel Hoists (1926.552) goes to Week 42.

Following your rule literally puts Hoists in Week 44, which leaves Week 42 needing a new topic. Two options not covered anywhere in the 52 weeks, with source text available:
1. **Working Over or Near Water** (1926.106: life jackets, ring buoys, lifesaving skiff). Pull pending.
2. **Crane Assembly/Disassembly** (1926.1403-1926.1406; 1926.1403 is already linked from Week 21).

Which goes in Week 42 (or another topic of your choice)?

**B. Recordkeeping in the 1926 track.** You said recordkeeping (Part 1904) belongs in both tracks. The replacement you approved for Week 48 ("Documentation and Record Keeping") was Hexavalent Chromium and Cadmium. Options:
1. Keep Week 48 as **Injury and Illness Recordkeeping (Part 1904)**, rewritten with 1904 citations, and drop the Cr(VI)/Cd replacement.
2. Keep Cr(VI)/Cd in Week 48 and put 1904 elsewhere.

Option 1 is the natural fit. 1926 Week 1 touches 1904.29, 1904.32, and 1904.39 only briefly, so a full week would not duplicate it.
